One farm, two disciplines, worked as a single system.

Nkwazi Farms began as a single maize plot on the outskirts of Lusaka. Today the land is split between open-field crops and grazing pasture, with manure from the herd feeding the soil and crop residue feeding the animals, a cycle rather than two separate businesses.

Every harvest and every herd is managed by the same family that started it, with agronomists and livestock officers brought in to keep both sides running to the same standard.

Our Process: From Field to Delivery

Every batch, whether crop or livestock, moves through the same six checkpoints before it leaves the farm.

01

Soil & Paddock Prep

Soil testing and paddock rest before the first rains, guided by an agronomist's recommendations.

02

Planting & Breeding

Crops go in on a rotation schedule as calving and breeding cycles begin.

03

Growing & Herd Care

Fields are tended and irrigated while herds rotate through pasture under regular veterinary checks.

04

Harvest & Selection

Crops are picked at peak ripeness; livestock is selected against weight and health benchmarks.

05

Sorting, Grading & Packing

Produce is graded by size and quality, then packed to the buyer's specification.

06

Delivery & Distribution

Orders move out on scheduled routes to markets, restaurants and wholesale buyers across the region.
